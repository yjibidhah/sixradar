02 / COMMON QUESTIONS

BEFORE YOU PLAY.

Does preloading mean I can play before November 19? +

No. Downloading early prepares the game files; the announced release is midnight local time on November 19, 2026.

What if the download doesn't start automatically? +

Check your purchase eligibility, free storage, automatic update settings, console power mode and game library. Rockstar links to official troubleshooting instructions for each platform.

Is GTA VI coming to PC on November 19? +

Rockstar currently lists PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S as the announced launch consoles. No PC launch date is confirmed in these sources.
