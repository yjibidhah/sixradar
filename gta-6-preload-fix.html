First: confirm your preload window

As of Rockstar's November 3 support update, digital preload begins November 10, 2026 at midnight local time on PS5 and November 10 at 00:00 GMT on Xbox Series X|S. These are different clock rules: a player outside GMT may see the Xbox option earlier or later on their own local calendar.

Check before troubleshooting

A missing download can simply mean that your region's preload time has not arrived. Use our regional timezone checker to understand the schedule, and ensure your edition is eligible for a digital preload.

PS5: game is not auto-downloading

  • Verify that the account holding the eligible digital pre-order is the account in use.
  • Confirm that November 10 at local midnight has passed in your area.
  • Enable automatic downloads using PlayStation's official pre-order support instructions.
  • Keep the PS5 powered on or in Rest Mode as Rockstar specifies.
  • If the game still does not appear, use PlayStation's official downloaded-game troubleshooting guide instead of unverified repair steps.

Xbox Series X|S: game is missing or queued

  • Confirm that the eligible Xbox purchase is associated with your signed-in Microsoft account.
  • Remember the stated worldwide start is 00:00 GMT on November 10—not midnight in every country.
  • Make sure automatic updates are enabled and the console is on or in Sleep mode.
  • Review the queue, storage space and any on-screen installation error.
  • Follow Xbox Support's installation troubleshooting steps if your download still fails.

What about physical boxes and region mismatches?

Rockstar states that physical GTA VI editions contain a digital download code instead of a game disc. Physical stock is scheduled from November 12, so you cannot start a code-based download before you actually receive and redeem that code. For PlayStation, Rockstar also documents region-specific code restrictions, so verify that your code matches your platform account region.

For digital orders, consult the correct store's purchase details before changing any account region or purchasing the game a second time.

If your installation shows an error

A general network or storage error is different from a preload not yet being offered. Take note of the error message and use the links above to your console maker's official installation support. We do not recommend risky third-party installers, leaked files or account-region workarounds.
