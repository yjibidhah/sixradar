Accuracy note

This is a timezone calculation, not a live connection to a console storefront. Rockstar specifies local midnight; final availability may vary if the publisher or platform updates its policy. Always check your own store for final unlock details.

The difference between release time and preload time

Preloading lets eligible customers download game files early; it does not unlock gameplay before the announced release. The PlayStation 5 preload starts at local midnight on November 10. Xbox Series X|S preloading starts at 00:00 GMT (UTC) on November 10, a single global instant.

Why a global launch has different local times

When Rockstar says “midnight local time” on November 19, the event follows each region's clock. For example, Auckland, London and Los Angeles do not share a single universal launch timestamp. Their own midnight arrives at different UTC instants.

Our converter uses named IANA timezones, so changes in daylight-saving time and half-hour or quarter-hour offsets are handled by the browser rather than guessed from country labels. It shows the computed moment in your current device timezone as well.

Why we ask for a city

Countries such as the United States, Canada and Australia span multiple time zones. Daylight-saving rules may also change during the year. Picking a city is more reliable than treating an entire country as one UTC offset.
