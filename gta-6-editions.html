Which GTA VI edition should you buy?

Rockstar lists Grand Theft Auto VI: Standard Edition and Ultimate Edition on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Standard includes the base game; Ultimate includes it plus an additional set of in-game items and features. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade can also be purchased separately by Standard owners.

A purchase tip

If you mainly want the base story and aren't sure about extra cosmetic or gameplay content, Standard is a straightforward choice. Rockstar says Standard owners can buy the Ultimate upgrade later. Compare the official local store prices before choosing.

What is the Vintage Vice City Pack?

Rockstar says eligible pre-orders or digital purchases before November 20 include the Vintage Vice City Pack, featuring a '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and Garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. Eligible digital purchases also include one month of GTA+ according to Rockstar's November 4 article.

Always check the specific store listing for the terms that apply in your country.

What does Ultimate add?

Rockstar lists an extra collection tied to Jason and Lucia's story. Examples include the '95 Grotti Cheetah, Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers, personalized weapon variants, Vice City styles, Jason's safehouse vehicles, and locations such as Ganado Retro Build, Rideout Customs Mod Shop and Sara's Unisex Salon.

Rockstar says more Ultimate items are encountered as the story progresses. The official edition details are the best source for the latest full list.

Does the physical GTA VI box contain a disc?

No. Rockstar states that the physical version ships with a code for a digital download—not a game disc. Availability is expected from November 12. That makes sufficient storage and the correct regional code important even for a physical purchase.

Rockstar says PlayStation download codes have region restrictions. Buy a code suitable for your existing account region rather than assuming any region's code will work.

Should you pay more for Ultimate?

Choose based on whether you'll actually use the extra content, not because a reseller suggests the Standard Edition is missing the main story. Both contain the core game. Prices and availability differ by storefront and market, so this page intentionally does not invent a universal price.
