Is GTA VI coming to PC on November 19?

Rockstar has confirmed PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S for the announced November 19, 2026 launch. Its official GTA VI page and November 4 platform support article do not announce a PC edition or a PC release date.

That is not evidence that GTA VI will never come to PC; it simply means the publisher has not provided a PC launch commitment in the currently cited official sources.

Avoid fake PC preorder links

Be suspicious of sites claiming to sell a “guaranteed GTA VI PC release-day key,” exclusive early-access PC downloads or definitive PC requirements. Verify an official PC announcement before spending money.

When is the GTA VI PC release date?

As of this guide's November 10, 2026 review, there is no officially announced PC release date. Predicting a month or year by comparing past Rockstar launches would be speculation, not a confirmed date. Any such prediction should be clearly labeled as a guess.

Are PC minimum requirements available?

No official GTA VI PC system-requirements sheet is published in Rockstar's current GTA VI game and support pages. Claims about required GPUs, processors, RAM or PC file sizes cannot be treated as verified PC requirements at this stage.

What about Steam and Epic Games?

The verified official GTA VI launch page currently highlights PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. SixRadar will not direct you to unofficial PC key sellers as if they were confirmed Rockstar-authorized launch stores. If an official PC listing appears in the future, confirm it through Rockstar's channels.

What can PC players do right now?

  • Follow Rockstar's GTA VI site and official Newswire for any platform announcement.
  • Treat PC-date videos and hardware-spec lists as unverified unless they cite a current publisher announcement.
  • Avoid buying third-party “PC GTA VI” keys before a legitimate PC edition is formally announced.
  • If you have a PS5 or Xbox Series X|S, use the currently announced console release and our official store links.
