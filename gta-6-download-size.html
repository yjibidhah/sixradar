GTA VI may need up to 212 GB

Rockstar Support states that Grand Theft Auto VI requires up to 212 GB of available space, depending on your device. That's the official maximum stated in its November 3 installation guidance—not a promise that every console download will be exactly 212 GB.

Preloading began on November 10 according to Rockstar. The final size shown in a platform's download queue may vary by device; do not confuse download transfer size, installed storage requirement, and temporary free space.

What to check now

Open your PS5 or Xbox storage settings and check available capacity before installing. If the console requests more free space, follow its on-screen guidance instead of relying on an unofficial file-size screenshot.

Does GTA VI really take 212 GB?

Not necessarily on every console. Rockstar's wording is “up to” 212 GB. It does not establish a single identical download size across all platforms or a definitive compressed transfer size. File-size rumors and unverified leaks should not override the official installation article.

Storage checklist before downloading

  • Check available storage under your console’s system settings.
  • Allow room for the game and any additional files your console requests.
  • If space is low, move or remove titles you no longer play; back up any important data first.
  • Keep automatic system and game updates enabled so eligible digital preloads can start.
  • Use the actual store download screen to confirm the size shown for your device.

Download size vs installed game

A network download can be compressed or delivered in parts, while the console may reserve additional space to install it. Rockstar currently provides an available-storage figure rather than separate official transfer-size figures for every console model.

Both digitally purchased editions use the platform download system. The physical “code-in-box” version contains a download code, not a playable disc, so you should still plan for a substantial download.

Need more help?

See the platform preload instructions if your download has not started. If it fails, use our preload troubleshooting checklist and follow your console manufacturer's support instructions.
